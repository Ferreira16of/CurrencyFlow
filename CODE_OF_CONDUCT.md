# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making CurrencyFlow welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.
- Keep discussions accessible and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed. To report a concern, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
